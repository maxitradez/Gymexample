Why being fit matters

Training isn’t about looking a certain way. It’s about what your body lets you do for the next forty years.

A stronger heart

Regular activity lowers the risk of heart disease, high blood pressure and type 2 diabetes.

Better sleep

People who train regularly tend to fall asleep faster and sleep more deeply.

A clearer mind

Exercise reduces stress and anxiety and helps lift low mood.

Strong bones

Lifting loads your bones, which helps protect against osteoporosis as you age.

More energy

A fitter body tires less, so the school run and the long workday feel lighter.

Independence

Strength is what lets you carry groceries, climb stairs and get off the floor at 80.

The World Health Organization suggests adults get 150 minutes of moderate activity a week. That’s 30 minutes, five days.
