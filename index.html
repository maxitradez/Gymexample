QUESTIONS?
WE'VE GOT YOU.

A few common questions before your first visit.

I'm new to the gym. Can I still join?

Absolutely. This demo is designed to welcome beginners as well as experienced members. A real gym can add details about its induction process and coaching support.

What should I bring for my first session?

Comfortable workout clothes, suitable shoes and water are a good start. Check with the gym for any specific requirements.

Can I visit before choosing a membership?

Use the enquiry form to ask about a tour or trial session. Availability and terms should be confirmed by the gym.

Do you offer personal training?

Personal training is shown as a sample service. Ask the gym about coach availability, session options and rates.
